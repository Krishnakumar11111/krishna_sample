Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

EduTrust Tuition Platform ("EduTrust", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of students, parents, tutors, and visitors who access our website, applications, and learning management systems. This policy explains how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide tailored tutoring and online educational services, including:

  • Account Details: Full name, email address, mobile number, user role (Student, Parent, or Teacher), and hashed passwords.
  • Academic Profile: Class, grade level, school board (CBSE, ICSE, State), subjects of interest, and study goals.
  • Tutor Verification: Educational qualifications, teaching certifications, years of experience, city/location, and optional profile photos.
  • Demo Booking Information: Preferred time slots, tuition mode (Home or Online), address details for in-person tuition, and learning objectives.
  • Platform Activity: Test results, homework submissions, attendance in live classes, and log analytics.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is utilized solely to:

  • Connect students with verified tutors based on subject, grade, and geographic proximity.
  • Schedule and manage free trial demo classes.
  • Facilitate batch enrollments and process payments via secure payment gateways.
  • Provide academic tracking, test scores, DPP performance, and homework evaluations.
  • Send relevant administrative notifications, class reminders, and safety updates.

4. Data Security & Storage

EduTrust implements robust technical and administrative security measures, including SSL encryption, database access restrictions, session timeout policies, and multi-factor authentication (MFA/OTP) for administrative accounts. We never sell or lease your personal information to third-party advertisers.

5. Your Rights & Contact Details

Users hold the right to review, update, or request the deletion of their personal profiles at any time. For questions or privacy requests, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@edutrust.in.
