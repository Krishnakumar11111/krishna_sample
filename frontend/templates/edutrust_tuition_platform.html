Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries regarding tutors, courses, payments, and registration.

You can easily search for verified local or online tutors by entering your locality, grade/class, and subject in the Search Tutors page. You can filter by tutor ratings, fees, and mode of tuition (home or online), browse complete tutor profiles, and request a free demo class directly.

Yes! A large portion of our curated notes, practice question papers, NCERT & ICSE solutions, and syllabus guides in our Study Material section are completely free for all registered students to download and read.

Teachers can click on the "Teacher Registration" or "Become a Tutor" button, fill out their educational background, teaching experience, preferred subjects/classes, and upload their credentials. Once verified, tutors can immediately browse and apply for tuition jobs in their preferred locations.

Yes, absolutely! EduTrust specializes in 1-on-1 personalized home tuition. Tutors visit your home at your scheduled convenient time. We also support hybrid and online 1-on-1 tuition options based on your preference.

We cater to students from Nursery/Kindergarten, Primary (Classes 1-5), Middle School (Classes 6-8), Secondary (Classes 9-10), and Senior Secondary (Classes 11-12) across CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, and IGCSE/IB, as well as competitive exam foundations like NEET and JEE.

Yes! EduTrust allows parents and students to book a 100% free, no-obligation demo session with shortlisted tutors. This helps you evaluate teaching methodology and student-teacher compatibility before finalizing regular tuition.

Every tutor undergoes a multi-step verification process: Government ID validation, academic degree/certificate checks, background assessment, and an initial teaching competency evaluation by our academic board.

Tuition fees depend on the student's class grade, educational board, subject complexity, weekly class hours, and whether the sessions are in-person or online. Tutors list their expected rates transparently on their profiles with no hidden charges.

Yes, student satisfaction is our top priority. If for any reason the tutor's pace or teaching style does not match your expectations, you can request a replacement tutor instantly through our support team at zero replacement fee.

If you don't find an exact match immediately, you can post your tuition requirements on our "Post Tuition Job" page or reach out to our student helpline directly. Our team will manually match and assign qualified tutors within 24 hours.
