1.1 Introduction to Real Numbers

In Class IX, you began your exploration of the world of real numbers and encountered irrational numbers. In this chapter, we continue our study of real numbers. We begin with two very important properties of positive integers, namely the Euclid’s division algorithm and the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic.

Theorem 1.1 (Euclid’s Division Lemma):

Given positive integers a and b, there exist unique integers q and r satisfying a = bq + r, 0 ≤ r < b.

Euclid’s division algorithm is a technique to compute the Highest Common Factor (HCF) of two given positive integers. Recall that the HCF of two positive integers a and b is the largest positive integer d that divides both a and b.
