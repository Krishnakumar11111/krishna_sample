Tutor Verification & Profile Information

  • EduTrust may review information provided by tutors to help maintain a trusted learning environment.
  • Tutor verification may include educational qualifications, certifications, teaching experience, subjects, and other relevant profile information.
  • Tutors may be required to provide supporting information or documents where applicable.
  • Information submitted for verification may be reviewed by authorized personnel for verification purposes.
  • Public tutor profiles should contain only information that is appropriate for helping students and parents make informed tutoring decisions.
  • Verification information should not be publicly displayed unless required or permitted for the relevant service.
