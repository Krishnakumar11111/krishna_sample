Physics • Class 12 Hard (3 Marks) Derivation
#Q-1049

State Gauss's law in electrostatics. Use it to derive an expression for the electric field due to an infinitely long straight uniformly charged wire.

Solution Blueprint: Construct a cylindrical Gaussian surface of radius $r$ and length $l$. $\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A} = E(2\pi rl) = \frac{\lambda l}{\epsilon_0} \implies E = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi \epsilon_0 r}$.
